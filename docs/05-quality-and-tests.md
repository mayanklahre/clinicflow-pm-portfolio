## Quality gates before real pilot
1. Permissioned privacy/vendor review and access controls.
2. Real UAT with 3–5 willing testers; actual SUS scores recorded.
3. Test booking, clash, cancel/reschedule, consent withdrawal, provider failure, outage fallback, and recovery.
4. Sponsor approval, staff training, cutover/rollback, and support ownership.
