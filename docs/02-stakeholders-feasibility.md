## Research plan

Conduct 2–3 brief interviews with front-desk staff and time at least five phone bookings. Ask: what information is collected, what causes delay, how are cancellations handled, when are reminders sent, and what makes a booking difficult to find. Obtain permission, remove identifiers, and record only anonymous themes. Replace illustrative figures only after observations are dated and logged.
