# Standard retrospective

**Retrospective documentation, written on 19 October 2026.** This links to an existing portfolio retrospective and does not create backdated delivery evidence.

## What worked
- The hybrid plan made business controls, Scrum delivery, privacy, testing, and rollout visible in one portfolio.
- Jira, Airtable, Figma, Make, GitHub, and evidence captions created an end-to-end traceability story.
- Fictitious data and test-only automation preserved the honesty/privacy boundary.

## What remains
- Replace illustrative baseline, UAT, and pilot assumptions with permissioned real evidence.
- Complete privacy, consent, vendor, access, and operational readiness gates before any real data or messages.
- Add production-grade slot locking if the workflow scales beyond the low-code proof of concept.
