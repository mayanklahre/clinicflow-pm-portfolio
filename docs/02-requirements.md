# Standard requirements summary

Detailed requirements and acceptance criteria are maintained in the [PRD](03-prd.md) and [Jira backlog](08-jira-backlog.md).

| Area | User need | Acceptance evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Booking | Select doctor/type, see future slots, provide minimum contact details, consent, and confirmation. | Acceptance criteria in Online Booking stories; fictional Airtable test record only. |
| Availability | Avoid use of unavailable slots and surface schedule changes. | Slot/status rules and manual retest evidence. |
| Front desk | View daily appointments and create phone bookings. | Dashboard/phone-booking stories and Figma frame. |
| Doctors | Manage availability and see own appointment schedule. | Doctor Schedule stories and Figma frame. |
| Reminders | Prepare consent-based 24-hour/2-hour reminder eligibility and delivery audit. | Test-only Make/Airtable design; no provider invoked. |
| Reporting | Review appointment outcomes and no-show rate. | No-show report wireframe and fictional-data evidence. |
| Quality | Validate with UAT and SUS. | UAT plan; any simulated results are labelled and cannot substitute for actual tester evidence. |
