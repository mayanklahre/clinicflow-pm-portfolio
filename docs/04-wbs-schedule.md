# Work breakdown structure and schedule

## WBS

| ID | Work package | Deliverable / exit condition |
|---|---|---|
| 1.0 | Initiate | Charter, stakeholder register, baseline research plan, feasibility check |
| 2.0 | Plan | PRD, WBS, schedule, budget, RACI, risks, controls, vendor decision |
| 3.0 | Sprint 1 | Booking, slot data model, consent, basic confirmation demonstrated |
| 4.0 | Sprint 2 | Reminder workflow, dashboard, schedules, core tests demonstrated |
| 5.0 | Sprint 3 | Reporting, hardening, UAT fixes, release readiness demonstrated |
| 6.0 | Go-live | UAT sign-off, training, cutover, pilot and rollback readiness |
| 7.0 | Close | Hypercare, benefits/KPI review, handover, retrospective, closure |

## Ten-week calendar

| Week | Dates | Focus | Milestone |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 3–9 Aug | Initiation and research plan | Charter baseline approved |
| 2 | 10–16 Aug | Planning and backlog refinement | Plan/budget baseline set |
| 3 | 17–23 Aug | Sprint 1, week 1 | Booking data model complete |
| 4 | 24–30 Aug | Sprint 1, week 2 | Sprint 1 review |
| 5 | 31 Aug–6 Sep | Sprint 2, week 1 | Reminder prototype complete |
| 6 | 7–13 Sep | Sprint 2, week 2 | Sprint 2 review |
| 7 | 14–20 Sep | Sprint 3, week 1 | Reporting/test execution |
| 8 | 21–27 Sep | Sprint 3, week 2 | Release candidate / UAT ready |
| 9 | 28 Sep–4 Oct | Go-live preparation and pilot | UAT sign-off and pilot |
| 10 | 5–11 Oct | Hypercare and closure | Handover and retrospective |

## Ceremonies

Planning/review/retrospective occur at each sprint boundary. A 15-minute weekly check-in covers progress, risks, blockers, change requests, and EV data. Product owner attends refinement and reviews; sponsor receives weekly status and approves material decisions.
