# Standard project brief

**Project:** ClinicFlow — Appointment Scheduling and No-Show Reduction  
**Delivery:** Self-initiated project-management portfolio, 3 August–11 October 2026. Not a paid client engagement; no patient data is used.

## Problem and users
A five-doctor clinic can lose time to phone-only booking, unclear front-desk visibility, and missed appointments. Intended users are patients, front-desk staff, doctors, clinic managers, and a privacy adviser.

## Goal and success criteria
Plan, configure, and validate a low-code MVP for booking, availability, front-desk visibility, reminders, schedules, and no-show reporting. Planned targets are derived from an illustrative baseline until permissioned observations replace them. The test-only reminder workflow must not send live messages.

## Scope
**In:** booking workflow, consent, slots, appointment list, doctor schedule, no-show report, low-code data modelling, Jira planning, UAT/pilot readiness.

**Out:** billing, electronic medical records, production clinical system replacement, live messaging before approval, and use of real patient data in the portfolio.
